A carousel steps through slides or images so people browse one item at a time without leaving the page. Focus stays on a single focal asset while order and paging remain explicit.

Prefer Carousel when sequence and focus matter; prefer a grid or list when every item should be visible without interaction.

## Best practices

**Make navigation obvious.** Previous, next, and optional dots or thumbnails should be visible — motion alone is not enough for everyone.

**Never autoplay aggressively.** If slides advance automatically, keep duration generous, pause on hover and focus, and respect reduced motion.

**Preserve position context.** Show index or progress (2 of 8) so people know how much remains.

**Keep slide content self-contained.** Each slide should make sense alone; do not split one sentence across two panels.

**Support keyboard and swipe.** Arrow keys and pointer swipes should both move slides predictably with no trapping focus away from the page.
