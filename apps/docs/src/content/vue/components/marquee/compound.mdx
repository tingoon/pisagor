## Anatomy

```vue
<Marquee.Root>
  <Marquee.Viewport>
    <Marquee.Content>
      <Marquee.Item />
    </Marquee.Content>
  </Marquee.Viewport>
  <Marquee.Edge side="start" />
  <Marquee.Edge side="end" />
</Marquee.Root>
```

Parts map 1:1 to Ark. The `Marquee` shorthand renders the viewport, items, and fading edges (`show-edges`) for you.
