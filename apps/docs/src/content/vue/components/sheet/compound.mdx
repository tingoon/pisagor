## Anatomy

```vue
<Sheet.Root>
  <Sheet.Trigger />
  <Sheet.Backdrop />
  <Sheet.Positioner>
    <Sheet.Content>
      <Sheet.Header>
        <Sheet.Title />
        <Sheet.Description />
      </Sheet.Header>
      <Sheet.Body />
      <Sheet.Footer>
        <Sheet.CloseTrigger />
      </Sheet.Footer>
    </Sheet.Content>
  </Sheet.Positioner>
</Sheet.Root>
```

## Examples

### Compound

Build the sheet from parts when the shorthand props are not enough. Wrap the backdrop and positioner in `Teleport`, and wrap `Sheet.Body` in `ScrollArea` when the body should scroll.
