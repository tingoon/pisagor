## Anatomy

```vue
<ImageCropper.Root>
  <ImageCropper.Viewport>
    <ImageCropper.Image />
    <ImageCropper.Selection>
      <ImageCropper.Grid />
      <ImageCropper.Handle />
    </ImageCropper.Selection>
  </ImageCropper.Viewport>
</ImageCropper.Root>
```

Parts map 1:1 to Ark (`Handle` renders its grip by default). The `ImageCropper` shorthand renders the viewport, image, grid lines, and all eight handles for you.
