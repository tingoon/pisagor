## Anatomy

```vue
<Popover.Root>
  <Popover.Trigger />
  <Popover.Anchor />
  <Popover.Positioner>
    <Popover.Content>
      <Popover.Arrow>
        <Popover.ArrowTip />
      </Popover.Arrow>
      <Popover.Header>
        <Popover.Title />
        <Popover.Description />
      </Popover.Header>
      <Popover.Body />
      <Popover.Footer />
      <Popover.CloseTrigger />
    </Popover.Content>
  </Popover.Positioner>
</Popover.Root>
```

## Examples

### Compound

Build the popover from parts when the shorthand props are not enough. Wrap the positioner in `Teleport`, and wrap `Popover.Body` in `ScrollArea` when the body should scroll.
