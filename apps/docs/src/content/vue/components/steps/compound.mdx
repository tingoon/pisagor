## Anatomy

```vue
<Steps.Root>
  <Steps.List>
    <Steps.Item>
      <Steps.Trigger>
        <Steps.Indicator />
        <Steps.Title />
        <Steps.Description />
      </Steps.Trigger>
      <Steps.Separator />
    </Steps.Item>
  </Steps.List>
  <Steps.Content />
  <Steps.CompletedContent />
  <Steps.PrevTrigger />
  <Steps.NextTrigger />
</Steps.Root>
```

Parts map 1:1 to Ark, plus `Title` / `Description` text regions. `Indicator` renders only its children (the shorthand adds the completed check). Pass your own buttons to `PrevTrigger` / `NextTrigger` with `as-child`; the `Steps` shorthand renders them for you.
