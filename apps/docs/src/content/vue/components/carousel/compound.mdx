## Anatomy

```vue
<Carousel.Root>
  <Carousel.Control>
    <Carousel.PrevTrigger />
    <Carousel.AutoplayTrigger>
      <Carousel.AutoplayIndicator />
    </Carousel.AutoplayTrigger>
    <Carousel.NextTrigger />
  </Carousel.Control>
  <Carousel.ItemGroup>
    <Carousel.Item />
  </Carousel.ItemGroup>
  <Carousel.IndicatorGroup>
    <Carousel.Indicator />
  </Carousel.IndicatorGroup>
</Carousel.Root>
```

Parts map 1:1 to Ark and render no buttons or icons (triggers keep localized `aria-label`s); pass a `Button` with `as-child`. The `Carousel` shorthand renders caret buttons for you.
