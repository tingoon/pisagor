## Anatomy

```vue
<Checkbox.Root>
  <Checkbox.Control>
    <Checkbox.Indicator />
  </Checkbox.Control>
  <Checkbox.Label />
  <Checkbox.HiddenInput />
</Checkbox.Root>
```

Parts map 1:1 to Ark and render no icons; put your check / minus icons in `Indicator`. The `Checkbox` shorthand renders them (plus an optional `label`) for you.
