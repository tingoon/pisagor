## Anatomy

```vue
<Pagination.Root>
  <Pagination.PrevTrigger />
  <Pagination.Context v-slot="{ pages }">
    <template v-for="(page, index) in pages" :key="index">
      <Pagination.Item v-if="page.type === 'page'" />
      <Pagination.Ellipsis v-else />
    </template>
  </Pagination.Context>
  <Pagination.NextTrigger />
</Pagination.Root>
```

Parts map 1:1 to Ark (`ItemLink` is a Pisagor helper for URL-driven pages). Pass your own buttons with `as-child` and your own icon inside `Ellipsis`; the `Pagination` shorthand renders the previous/next buttons and page list for you.
