## Anatomy

```tsx
<Collapsible.Root>
  <Collapsible.Trigger>
    <Collapsible.Indicator />
  </Collapsible.Trigger>
  <Collapsible.Content>
    <Collapsible.Body />
  </Collapsible.Content>
</Collapsible.Root>
```

Parts map 1:1 to Ark, plus `Body` for the padded region inside `Content`. The `Collapsible` shorthand renders the trigger label, indicator, and body for you.
