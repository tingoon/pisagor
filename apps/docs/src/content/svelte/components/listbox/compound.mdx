## Anatomy

```tsx
<Listbox.Root>
  <Listbox.Content>
    <Listbox.Item>
      <Listbox.ItemText />
      <Listbox.ItemIndicator />
    </Listbox.Item>
  </Listbox.Content>
</Listbox.Root>
```

Parts map 1:1 to Ark and render no icons; put your check icon inside `ItemIndicator`.
