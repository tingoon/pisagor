Vue `Collapsible` follows the thin parts + shorthand pattern (mirrors React). `Collapsible` is now a shorthand (`trigger`, `showIndicator`, `classNames`); adds `Collapsible.Root` and `Collapsible.Body`. **Breaking:** compound usage starts from `Collapsible.Root` (`<Collapsible>` was the root before); `Indicator` no longer renders a caret icon; compose your own.
