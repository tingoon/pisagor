Vue `InputOTP` follows the thin parts + shorthand pattern (mirrors React). `InputOTP` is a shorthand (`length`, `groupSize`, `classNames`); adds `InputOTP.Root`, `Label`, `Control`, `Input`, `Separator`. `vue-form` `OtpField` uses the compound parts. **Breaking:** `Slot` was renamed to `Input`.
