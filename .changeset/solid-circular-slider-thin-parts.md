Solid `CircularSlider` follows the thin parts + shorthand pattern (mirrors React). The shorthand gains `label`, `showValue`, `valuePrefix`, `valueSuffix` and `classNames`; new parts `Root`, `Label`, `Ring`, `HiddenInput` and `Context`. **Breaking:** `CircularSlider.Control` no longer renders the ring, markers and thumb; `CircularSlider.ValueText` no longer takes `prefix`/`suffix`; named `CircularSliderRoot`, … exports are removed.
